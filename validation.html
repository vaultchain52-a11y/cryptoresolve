Validation and Integrity Checks

Every wallet recovery request runs through multiple validation layers to ensure authenticity, format correctness, and chain compliance.

Phrase Validation

Validate that recovery phrases match accepted BIP39 wordlists and are structured correctly before processing.

Keystore & Key Checks

Verify keystore JSON, encryption headers, and private key formats against supported wallet specifications.

Chain Address Proof

Confirm wallet addresses and chain IDs are valid to avoid cross-chain or malformed recovery attempts.

Why Validation Matters

  • Invalid or malformed inputs are rejected before any recovery action begins.
  • Wallet metadata is validated to match the selected recovery method and network.
  • Trusted validation prevents accidental use of unsupported wallet types.

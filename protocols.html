Protocols for Secure Blockchain Recovery

CryptoResolve leverages authentication, node validation, and synced ledger protocols to recover and verify wallet access across decentralized networks.

Network Integrity

Validate node connections against consensus and ensure wallet recovery commands pass through authenticated blockchain endpoints only.

Encrypted Transmission

Every recovery payload is encrypted using AES-256 and sent over TLS-protected channels to prevent interception and tampering.

Multi-Chain Support

Our protocol stack supports Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, and leading smart-contract networks for broad wallet compatibility.

How Protocols Protect Recovery

  • Protocol versioning prevents stale or deprecated recovery flows from being used.
  • Session fingerprinting binds each recovery request to a trusted browser context.
  • Replay protection blocks repeated requests and reduces attack surface on wallet recovery endpoints.
